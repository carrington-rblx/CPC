What we've delivered

Results that matter for Canadians. Here's what Conservatives have achieved and continue to fight for.

Standing up for Canadian families

From tax relief to stronger communities, the Conservative Party has consistently delivered policies that put ordinary Canadians first.

  • Championing lower taxes so families keep more of what they earn
  • Fighting for safer streets and communities across the country
  • Supporting resource development and energy independence
  • Defending free speech and democratic institutions
  • Pushing for real action on housing affordability

A record of opposition that works

As Official Opposition, Conservatives have held the government accountable, forced better legislation, and offered a clear alternative focused on growth, safety, and opportunity.
